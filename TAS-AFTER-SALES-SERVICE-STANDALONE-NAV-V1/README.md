# TAS After Sales Service Standalone Nav V1

Moves the existing TAS service booking entry out of the Automotive sidebar group and makes it a standalone root-level sidebar item.

Display:
- Arabic: خدمة ما بعد البيع
- English: After Sales Service
- Route: /tas/service

Permissions are preserved:
Admin, admin, SalesManager, ServiceAdvisor, CrmFollowUp.

No backend, database, routing, booking-flow, or Help Center behavior is changed.
OpenHands must not commit or push.
